## Test quality assessment

Strong on the paths that matter most. Eight of eleven mutations I introduced were caught, including
every rejection path the story exists to provide (unknown key, key absent from default, missing key,
env precedence). `test_loads_into_a_frozen_dataclass` asserts the frozen property directly rather
than assuming it.

Two weaknesses:

1. Type validation is tested for exactly one of five branches. `_convert`'s `bool`, `str`, and
   `list/tuple` guards can all be deleted with the suite staying green (M26/M27/M28). For a module
   whose entire job is validation, that is a meaningful gap in the safety net rather than a
   cosmetic one.
2. `test_repository_configuration_loads:105-109` mixes real assertions (`normalization_form == "NFC"`,
   `"body" in region_kinds`) with truthiness ones (`assert config.charset.unknown_token`,
   `assert config.metrics.worst_n > 0`). The latter are the `len() > 0` pattern the checklist calls
   out — they would pass against almost any value.
